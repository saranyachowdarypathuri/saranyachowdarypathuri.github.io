About me

I am Saranya Chowdary Pathuri, a Software Developer with over 7 years of experience designing and building scalable, secure, and cloud-native applications across fintech, healthcare, and retail domains. My work focuses on creating reliable, high-performance systems that power complex business workflows and deliver seamless digital experiences.

I specialize in Java,Spring Boot and React.js, with a strong foundation in distributed microservices, RESTful APIs, and event-driven architectures using Kafka and RabbitMQ. I am passionate about building software that not only performs well but is also maintainable, testable, and future-ready.

Over the years, I have worked with teams to build cloud-driven solutions, automate deployments, and uphold high standards of code quality and collaboration. I believe in writing clean, efficient code, practicing TDD, and continuously improving engineering standards through collaboration and mentorship.

Beyond coding, I enjoy solving real-world problems through technology, mentoring junior developers, and exploring new frameworks that push the boundaries of scalability and usability.
